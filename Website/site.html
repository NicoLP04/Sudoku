OCR Sudoku

An OCR Sudoku, or Optical Character Recognition Sudoku, involves using technology to interpret and solve Sudoku puzzles.
Here is how it works :

Preprocessing

Preprocessing is an important step that involves preparing the image before the actual character recognition can take place. The preprocessing is crucial as it directly impacts the accuracy and efficiency of the OCR system in recognizing and solving the Sudoku puzzle.

Grid detection

Grid detection in OCR Sudoku involves identifying and isolating the grid structure that contains the individual cells of the puzzle. This step is essential for accurately segmenting the puzzle into its constituent parts. Once the grid structure is detected and isolated, the individual cells containing digits can be extracted for further processing and OCR recognition.

Grid cropping and tiles detection

After the grid detection step in OCR Sudoku, the focus shifts to grid cropping and tiles detection, which involves isolating individual cells or tiles containing the digits of the puzzle grid. These techniques work together to accurately crop and detect the individual tiles or cells within the Sudoku grid, preparing them for further processing such as digit recognition through OCR algorithms.

Grid reconstruction

Grid reconstruction in OCR Sudoku involves assembling the recognized digits back into the grid structure to reconstruct the entire Sudoku puzzle after individual cell extraction and digit recognition. This step is crucial to reassemble the solved puzzle or display the recognized digits in their correct positions.

Finding the solution

Solving a Sudoku puzzle involves using logic and deduction to fill in the empty cells with the correct numbers while adhering to the rules of Sudoku.

Print the solved result

After solving an OCR Sudoku puzzle, printing the solution involves displaying or formatting the solved puzzle grid in a readable and understandable format. The goal of printing the solution in OCR Sudoku is to provide users with a clear and accurate representation of the solved puzzle, allowing them to verify the solution or use it for their reference or enjoyment.
